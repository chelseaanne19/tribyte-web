# Privacy, moderation and responsible publication

**Purpose:** Protect contributors and readers while keeping the initial student project simple. This is a product checklist, not legal advice or a claim of compliance certification.

## Initial rule: curated content only

- Initial website is **read-only** for recommendations. Students give tips to the team in person or via an explicitly approved research process; a team member reviews/edit-publishes them manually.
- Do **not** add a public 'submit and instantly publish' button, anonymous posting feed, or third-party public storage until the team can moderate and respond to removal requests.
- Only publish real contributors' wording/photos with **explicit permission** for that exact use. Photos are optional and should be avoided in the first public guide unless specifically needed and permissioned.
- Provide a simple, visible method to request corrections/removal before a public launch (e.g. team-managed contact channel; don't invent a contact address in the code).

## Consent and identity

- Tell contributors what will be public, whether their name/alias appears, how long content may remain, and how they can request removal.
- Prefer public labels like **'Exchange student'** or an approved alias. Never expose phone numbers, student IDs, personal email addresses, housing addresses or private whereabouts.
- Keep private consent records and raw interview notes somewhere controlled **outside public GitHub**; limit who in the team can access them.
- Publishing names, photos or quotes requires separate permission. Consent for a research interview is not automatically consent for public publication.

## Factual accuracy and safety

- Check public place names and coordinates. Do not publish private home addresses or intimate personal locations.
- Public transport, fares, service times, discounts and other changing information must be dated, caveated and linked to authoritative sources if asserting specifics. Re-review or remove stale claims.
- Remove discriminatory content, targeted harassment, spam, unsafe advice, doxxing and copyright-infringing materials.
- Clearly label subjective recommendations as **student experiences**, not verified official UCD or transport-provider endorsement.

## Postcard privacy

- MVP postcard upload/preview/export works inside the browser. Selected photos must not be sent to TriByte servers or added to analytics.
- Users download their own PNG and decide where to share it. Never silently post or store an image publicly.
- Explain session limitations, including that reloading may erase unfinished edits.

## If a contribution form is added later

This needs a separate technical/privacy review and an owner willing to moderate. Minimum controls:

- Submission does **not** auto-publish; review queue and clear public/private status.
- Rate limiting/spam protection appropriate to the platform.
- Clear notice of collection, retention, review and withdrawal/removal process.
- Ability to delete content and handle reports.
- Only request fields actually needed. Avoid requiring a public profile for one tip.
- Define who approves posts and who responds to safety reports after the module ends.

## Before public launch checklist

- [ ] Every visible community recommendation is genuine, approved or **clearly flagged fictional demo**.
- [ ] No private contributor details or consent documents in the repository.
- [ ] All pins have real verified public locations.
- [ ] Time-sensitive claims are dated or linked to current official sources.
- [ ] A corrections/removal contact method exists and is monitored.
- [ ] Site accurately describes photo processing and any cookies/analytics.
- [ ] Map imagery, photographs, fonts and branded elements are licensed/attributed.
